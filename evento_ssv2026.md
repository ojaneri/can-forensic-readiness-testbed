## Historical fit

SSV 2024 and 2025 accepted applied works on vehicular platforms, simulation, dataset generation, multisensor synchronization, V2X, VANETs, ECU data acquisition, ADAS, and anomaly detection. This suggests that a practical and reproducible software-defined CAN testbed is a good fit.

## Strategic implication

The paper should not read like a pure survey. It should deliver:

1. A reproducible low-cost CAN testbed architecture.
2. Controlled attack scenarios and CAN traffic logs.
3. Evidence-preserving logging with hashes and manifests.
4. A comparison with existing testbeds and datasets.
5. A clear discussion of limitations and hardware-in-the-loop extension.
