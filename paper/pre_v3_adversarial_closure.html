Executive decision

The paper is now stronger than v2, but v3 should reorganize the story instead of merely appending artifacts.

A low-cost software-defined CAN testbed can support reproducible cybersecurity education and forensic-readiness workflows by generating labeled internal experiments, preserving evidence packages, ingesting external traces, and safely replaying/injecting didactic events for review.

Safe framing: reproducible testbed, evidence workflow, external-trace compatibility, didactic spoof injection, safe observer demos.

Avoid framing as: IDS benchmark, real-vehicle attack, physical CAN fidelity, or robust detection paper.
