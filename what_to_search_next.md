## Research gaps

1. **Forensic-by-design testbeds:** many testbeds focus on attacks or IDS, but not evidence preservation.
2. **Software-defined reproducibility:** hardware testbeds may be expensive or difficult to replicate.
3. **Education + forensic readiness:** the combination is still underexplored.
4. **Clear limitations:** software-only simulation needs explicit boundaries and an HIL extension path.
5. **Latin-American relevance:** low-cost training environments are relevant to emerging automotive markets.
